Scion Project

Privacy: crash reports

Crash and boot reports are planned, and they are opt-in. Nothing is sent unless you say yes.

The question

The installer asks one question: "Send anonymous crash and boot reports to help Scion? Yes / No". You can change the answer later.

What a report contains

  • A summary of a crash or a boot: what failed, and where.
  • The build's hashes, so a report can be matched to the image that made it.
  • The phone model.
  • A random install ID.

Nothing else is in a report.

What is removed on the phone first

Before a report leaves the phone, it is scrubbed there of:

  • serial numbers and the IMEI;
  • MAC and Bluetooth addresses;
  • Wi-Fi network names;
  • IP addresses;
  • email addresses and phone numbers;
  • location;
  • anything else that identifies the device.

Where it goes, and how long it is kept

Reports are sent over HTTPS to https://claudiusthebot.duckdns.org/scion/report. The server does not store IP addresses. Reports are deleted after 90 days.

Changing your answer

Say No at install, or change the answer later. Nothing is sent unless the answer is Yes.
